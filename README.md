My name is Constance Bello. I'm a rebel technologist with a B.S. in Computer Science from Fordham University. My technical interests include privacy, security, and anonymity; the relationship between technology and disability justice; and how technology can be used for the interests of the people instead of the powerful. I am also deeply invested in sharing what I know with others, building community, and meaningfully contributing to social movements.
