My name is Constance Bello. I'm a New York City-based rebel technologist working at the intersection of software and social movements, developing applications and education to protect the public from incursions by the tech oligarchy into their lives. I am particularly interested in issues of individuals' privacy, security, and anonymity in the era of surveillance capitalism, and my creations are mindful of how problems around access, inequity, and historical disparities shape public understanding of technology. I can be reached at [me@constancebello.dev](mailto:me@constancebello.dev).
